Adjusting Screw

Versatile Mechanical Calibration

Reliable Everyday Adjustments

The Standard Adjusting Screw is the foundational component for reliable mechanical calibration across countless industries. Designed for versatility and everyday durability, it allows operators to precisely set tensions, clearances, and alignments in standard machinery and structural assemblies.

Manufactured with high-quality steel and strict thread tolerances, our standard adjusting screws ensure smooth actuation without galling or binding. Whether paired with a jam nut for secure locking or utilized in a tapped housing, they provide a cost-effective, dependable solution for holding exact positions over time.

Technical Specifications

  • Material Options: Carbon Steel (Grade 4.8 or 8.8) and 304 Stainless Steel for excellent baseline durability and strength.
  • Surface Finish: Bright Zinc Plated (BZP) to resist rust and corrosion in standard indoor or mildly exposed environments.
  • Thread Types: Available in Standard Metric (M4 to M24) and UNC/UNF sizes to fit universal industrial tapping.
  • Head Configurations: Slotted hex, standard hex, or socket set screw styles tailored for varied wrench or screwdriver access.
  • Tip Styles: Flat point, cup point, or dog point available depending on whether you need flush contact, biting grip, or guided alignment.

Industry Applications

Our standard adjusting screws are a staple in general manufacturing and assembly applications:

  • Conveyor Systems: Fine-tuning drive belt tensions and adjusting width guides on packaging lines.
  • Electric Motors & Pumps: Aligning base plates and setting proper pulley tensions.
  • Manufacturing Jigs & Fixtures: Acting as adjustable stop-blocks for repeatable workpiece positioning.
  • General Assembly: Setting operational clearances in HVAC louvers, brackets, and light automotive assemblies.
