Industrial Bins & Hoppers

Heavy-Duty Bulk Storage

Secure Storage & Smooth Discharge

Our industrial bins and hoppers are engineered for the safe handling, storage, and seamless discharge of bulk dry materials, powders, granules, and heavy manufactured parts. Built with reinforced structural framing, they prevent bulging and structural fatigue even under maximum load capacities.

Designed with highly engineered mass-flow bottoms (conical or sloped), our bins eliminate material bridging and rat-holing, ensuring a consistent, controlled flow during discharge. Smooth interior welds guarantee that no product gets trapped, making them easy to clean and highly sanitary.

Technical Specifications

  • Material Construction: 304/316L Stainless Steel for sanitary needs, or Heavy-Gauge Carbon Steel for raw industrial storage.
  • Bottom Designs: Symmetrical conical, asymmetric sloped, or flat-bottom designs depending on flow requirements.
  • Mobility & Handling: Integrated heavy-duty forklift channels, crane lifting lugs, or lockable polyurethane casters.
  • Discharge Control: Manual slide gates, pneumatic butterfly valves, or rotary airlock feeders.
  • Capacities: Custom fabricated from small 50-liter mobile transfer bins to massive 10,000+ liter stationary silos.

Industry Applications

Built for absolute durability, our bins provide vital material staging and storage across diverse sectors:

  • Food & Pharmaceuticals: Staging raw dry ingredients, pharmaceutical powders, and grain storage.
  • Plastics & Chemical: Bulk resin pellet storage and dry chemical powder containment.
  • Agriculture & Milling: Seed, feed, and flour storage with moisture-resistant sealing.
  • Manufacturing & Scrap: Heavy-duty bins for metal shavings, offcuts, and recycling sorting.
