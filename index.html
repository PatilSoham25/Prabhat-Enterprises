CUSTOM STAINLESS STEEL TANK, VESSEL, & EQUIPMENT FABRICATOR

SFI has been building stainless steel tanks and equipment since 1985, working with our customers to provide solutions for their stainless tank requirements. We continue to grow and evolve to meet the demands of a wide variety of industries and you can trust that your tank project will be completed with the expert knowledge and skill of the entire SFI team.

SFI has the experience needed for a variety of tank projects, ranging from a few custom-fabricated storage tanks to large multi-million dollar tank projects for both facility expansions and new construction. We have successfully completed thousands of jobs since opening our doors, currently employing about 200 people, many of whom have worked for the company for 25 years or more.

Using 300 series stainless steel and various other nickel alloys, SFI fabricates tanks in both the shop and in the field. With experience navigating complex projects and constructing tanks up to 750,000 gallons, SFI can build what you need, when you need it, and even where you are.
