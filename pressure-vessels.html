Industrial Pressure Vessels

Pressure Vessel

Engineered for Extreme Conditions

Our industrial pressure vessels are custom-engineered to safely contain liquids, vapors, and gases at pressures and temperatures significantly different from ambient conditions. Because the stakes are incredibly high, absolute structural integrity is our primary focus.

Every vessel is fabricated with precision-rolled steel and expertly welded to eliminate stress concentrations. Whether you need a simple compressed air receiver or a complex, multi-chamber chemical reactor, our vessels are rigorously tested to ensure fail-safe operation and zero-leak performance under extreme cyclical loads.

Technical Specifications

  • Certifications: Fully compliant with ASME Boiler and Pressure Vessel Code (BPVC) Section VIII, Division 1 & 2.
  • Material Construction: Carbon Steel, 304/316L Stainless Steel, Duplex Stainless, and high-nickel alloys.
  • Pressure Ratings: Engineered to handle extreme conditions, from full vacuum to 3,000+ PSI.
  • Quality Testing: 100% Non-Destructive Testing (NDT), including radiography (X-ray), ultrasonic, and hydrostatic testing.
  • Custom Fittings: Sight glasses, manways, flanged nozzles, and specialized pressure relief valve (PRV) mounts.

Industry Applications

Designed for absolute containment and safety, our pressure vessels are the critical core of many high-stakes industrial processes:

  • Oil & Gas Processing: Separators, knock-out drums, and compressed natural gas (CNG) storage.
  • Chemical & Petrochemical: High-pressure chemical reactors, distillation columns, and pressurized holding tanks.
  • Power Generation: Steam drums, surge tanks, and boiler feedwater heaters.
  • Pneumatic & Hydraulic Systems: Heavy-duty compressed air receivers and pressurized fluid reservoirs.
