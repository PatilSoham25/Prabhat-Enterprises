Industrial Trolleys & Carts

Trolley

Ergonomic & High-Capacity Transport

Our industrial trolleys are engineered to optimize material handling while minimizing operator fatigue and workplace injuries. Standard commercial carts buckle under heavy industrial use, but our trolleys feature fully welded tubular steel or stainless steel frames designed to withstand massive dynamic loads and daily impacts.

Equipped with premium industrial-grade casters, they provide exceptionally smooth rolling resistance even when fully loaded. Whether you are moving heavy machine parts across a factory floor or transporting sensitive sterile equipment in a cleanroom, our trolleys deliver reliable, highly maneuverable performance.

Technical Specifications

  • Material Options: 304/316L Stainless Steel for sanitary applications, or Heavy-Gauge Powder-Coated Steel for raw manufacturing.
  • Load Capacities: Engineered to handle extreme weights, from 500 lbs (light duty) up to 5,000+ lbs (heavy industrial duty).
  • Deck Styles: Smooth flatbeds, anti-slip diamond tread, wire mesh, or custom raised-lip trays to contain liquid spills.
  • Caster Systems: High-impact polyurethane, non-marking rubber, or high-temp phenolic wheels. Features dual-locking swivel mechanisms.
  • Configurations: Single-tier platform, multi-shelf utility, enclosed cage trolleys, and specialized cylinder carts.

Industry Applications

Built for absolute durability and precise maneuverability, our trolleys are essential to workflow efficiency across multiple sectors:

  • Manufacturing & Automotive: Moving heavy engine blocks, tooling dies, and bulk raw materials across shop floors.
  • Pharmaceuticals & Cleanrooms: Crevice-free, electro-polished stainless trolleys for sterile environments.
  • Food & Beverage Processing: Washdown-safe transport carts for meat packing, baking racks, and ingredient batching.
  • Warehousing & Logistics: High-capacity picking carts and secure cage trolleys for order fulfillment.
