Adjusting Screw 119

High-Precision Industrial Fasteners

Precision Mechanical Clearance

The Adjusting Screw 119 is a specialized, high-tensile component engineered to set and maintain precise mechanical clearances in high-vibration environments. Unlike standard fasteners, this screw acts as a critical calibration point, ensuring optimal performance and longevity of complex mechanical assemblies.

Manufactured locally with strict quality controls, the 119 series features a specially heat-treated contact point to resist wear under constant friction. Whether used in heavy-duty valve lash adjustments or precision flow control systems, it guarantees absolute stability once locked into position.

Technical Specifications

  • Material: Premium Alloy Steel (Grade 8 / 10.9 equivalent) for maximum tensile strength.
  • Surface Treatment: Black oxide finish or Zinc-Nickel plating for superior corrosion resistance in harsh industrial environments.
  • Tip Hardness: Induction-hardened contact tip (HRC 50-55) to prevent deformation under repetitive mechanical impact.
  • Thread Profile: Precision-rolled fine threads designed to resist loosening caused by high-frequency vibrations.
  • Included Hardware: Comes standard with a matched, high-torque locking jam nut.

Industry Applications

Built for rigorous mechanical calibration, the Adjusting Screw 119 is heavily utilized in sectors requiring zero-tolerance performance:

  • Automotive & Diesel Engines: Critical for setting valve lash clearances in heavy-duty internal combustion engines.
  • Hydraulic Systems: Serving as highly accurate flow and pressure calibration screws in industrial pumps.
  • Heavy Machinery: Calibration points for industrial presses, CNC machinery, and automated assembly lines.
  • Pneumatic Tools: Governor and torque adjustment mechanisms in high-powered pneumatic equipment.
