Technical Insight: Why Our Adjusting Screws?

Unlike standard bolts, industrial adjusting screws (often setscrews) are typically headless and fully threaded. They work by passing through a threaded hole in one component and exerting strong compression forces against a second component shaft or surface.

We fabricate our screws from 300-series stainless steel, ensuring rust resistance and durability. They are critical for machinery alignment, securing components like pulleys or gears to shafts, and calibration mechanisms where protrusions-free design is essential.
