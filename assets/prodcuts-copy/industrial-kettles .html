Industrial Kettles

Precision Heating & Cooking

Our industrial jacketed kettles are engineered for rapid, uniform heating and cooking of liquid and semi-solid products. The defining feature of these vessels is their hemispherical bottom, which provides superior heat transfer and eliminates the "burn-on" dead zones found in flat-bottomed tanks.

By surrounding the inner vessel with a double-wall jacket, steam, hot water, or thermal fluid is circulated to gently and evenly cook the product. Paired with our custom scraped-surface agitators, these kettles keep viscous products moving, preventing thermal degradation and ensuring a perfect batch every time.

Technical Specifications

  • Jacket Types: Conventional steam jacket, dimple jacket, or half-pipe coil.
  • Working Capacity: 10-gallon pilot kettles up to massive 1,000+ gallon production vessels.
  • Agitator Design: Scraped-surface, double-motion, or inclined agitators with Teflon/nylon scrapers.
  • Discharge Systems: Hydraulic tilt-out designs or flush-mounted bottom ball valves.
  • Pressure Ratings: ASME-certified jackets rated from 45 PSI to 150+ PSI.

Industry Applications

Designed for easy clean-in-place (CIP) sanitation and extreme thermal efficiency, our kettles are the backbone of commercial cooking and processing:

  • Food & Culinary: Soups, stews, gravies, pie fillings, jams, jellies, and caramel.
  • Beverage: Brewing mash tuns, syrup preparation, and pasteurization.
  • Cosmetics: Melting waxes, lip balms, lotions, and heavy creams.
  • Pharmaceuticals: Medicated ointments, gel capsules, and sterile heated suspensions.
