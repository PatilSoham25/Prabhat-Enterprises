Stainless Steel Mixing Tanks

High-Efficiency Blending & Agitation

Our stainless steel mixing tanks are engineered to deliver uniform blending, rapid emulsification, and perfect suspension for a variety of liquid and semi-solid formulations. Designed for total reliability, they minimize batch times and maximize product consistency.

Whether you require gentle low-shear folding or aggressive high-shear homogenization, we custom-build the tank and agitator combination to suit your precise fluid dynamics.

Technical Specifications

  • Material Options: 304, 316, and 316L Pharmaceutical-Grade Stainless Steel.
  • Capacity Range: From 50-gallon pilot tanks up to 10,000-gallon mass production vessels.
  • Agitation Types: Anchor, Turbine, Propeller, Scraped Surface, or High-Shear Rotor/Stator.
  • Heating/Cooling: Optional dimple jackets or half-pipe coil jackets for precise temperature control.
  • Interior Finish: Standard 2B mill finish up to ultra-sanitary < 15 Ra mechanical polish.

Industry Applications

Built to strictly adhere to ASME, API, and sanitary 3-A standards, these versatile mixing tanks are heavily utilized across multiple rigorous sectors:

  • Food & Beverage: Syrups, sauces, dairy processing, and brewing.
  • Chemical & Petrochemical: Resins, polymers, adhesives, and fertilizer blending.
  • Pharmaceutical & Cosmetics: Medical suspensions, lotions, and sterile saline solutions.
  • Water Treatment: Coagulant mixing and chemical dosing tanks.
