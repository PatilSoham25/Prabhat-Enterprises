Industrial Manhole Covers

Manhole Covers

Secure Access & Extreme Load Bearing

Our industrial manhole covers are engineered to provide safe, reliable access to underground utilities, drainage networks, and storage tanks. Built to withstand punishing environments, these covers ensure that massive surface loads from heavy machinery and vehicles are safely distributed without compromising the infrastructure below.

Unlike standard municipal covers, our industrial range includes precision-machined seating to prevent rattling under traffic, integrated anti-slip surface profiles to protect workers, and specialized locking mechanisms to prevent unauthorized access or accidental displacement during heavy flooding.

Technical Specifications

  • Material Construction: High-strength Ductile Iron, Heavy-Duty Cast Iron, or 316L Stainless Steel for corrosive environments.
  • Load Ratings: Certified from Class B125 (pedestrian/light vehicle) up to Class F900 (extreme loads, airports, and dockyards).
  • Sealing Options: Available in double-sealed watertight and gastight configurations to trap noxious odors and prevent liquid ingress.
  • Locking Mechanisms: High-security bolt-down designs, hinged security locks, and spring-bar mechanisms.
  • Surface Profiles: Raised checker plate, recessed for concrete/tile filling, or custom slotted for rapid surface drainage.

Industry Applications

Designed for absolute durability and safety, our manhole covers protect critical subterranean networks across demanding sectors:

  • Manufacturing & Warehousing: Forklift-rated covers for heavy factory floors and loading docks.
  • Petrochemical & Refining: Gastight and spark-resistant stainless covers for volatile chemical containment areas.
  • Municipal Water & Sewage: Heavy-duty, odor-blocking access points for deep sewage lines and water treatment plants.
  • Aviation & Ports: Ultra-heavy-duty F900 covers capable of supporting airplane wheel loads and massive shipping container handlers.
