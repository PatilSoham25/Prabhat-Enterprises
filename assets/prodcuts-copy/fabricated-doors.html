Industrial Fabricated Doors

Heavy-Duty Steel Access Doors

Absolute Security & Environmental Control

Our industrial fabricated doors are custom-engineered to provide unyielding security, weather resistance, and heavy-duty operational reliability. Standard commercial doors quickly fail in harsh factory environments, which is why our doors feature continuously welded seamless frames and reinforced internal steel stiffeners to prevent warping or buckling under extreme impact.

Whether you need an oversized sliding door for forklift access, a hermetically sealed cleanroom door, or a highly rated fire door to protect critical infrastructure, we fabricate each unit to perfectly match your facility's precise architectural and safety requirements.

Technical Specifications

  • Material Construction: 16 to 12-gauge cold-rolled steel, galvanized steel, or 304/316L stainless steel for maximum corrosion resistance.
  • Core Options: Honeycomb for impact resistance, polystyrene/polyurethane for thermal insulation, or mineral board for acoustic and fire ratings.
  • Hardware Integration: Factory-prepped for heavy-duty ball bearing hinges, panic crash bars, hydraulic closers, and biometric security locks.
  • Custom Configurations: Single-leaf, double-leaf, sliding, and blast-resistant models available.
  • Finishes: Standard shop primer, industrial powder coating, or marine-grade epoxy paints.

Industry Applications

Built to withstand daily abuse and protect vital assets, our fabricated doors are installed in the most demanding environments:

  • Manufacturing & Warehousing: High-traffic impact doors for loading docks, forklift routes, and heavy assembly floors.
  • Pharmaceuticals & Food: Crevice-free, stainless steel sanitary doors for washdown environments and cleanrooms.
  • Energy & Power Plants: Acoustic-rated and fire-rated doors to contain loud generator rooms and hazardous materials.
  • Commercial Security: Reinforced access points for server rooms, secure vaults, and perimeter facility exits.
