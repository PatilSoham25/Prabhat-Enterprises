Industrial Blenders

Homogeneous Dry & Wet Blending

Our industrial blenders are the ultimate solution for achieving perfectly homogeneous mixtures of dry powders, granules, and heavy pastes. Unlike liquid mixing tanks, these horizontal and V-shaped blenders are specifically engineered to move dense, resistant materials with precision.

Featuring highly engineered internal agitators—such as double-helical ribbons or sweeping paddles—our blenders ensure continuous lateral and radial movement, eliminating dead spots and dramatically reducing batch times.

Technical Specifications

  • Blender Types: Ribbon Blenders, Paddle Blenders, and V-Cone Powder Blenders.
  • Working Capacity: Ranging from 5 cubic feet (lab scale) to 500+ cubic feet (mass production).
  • Agitator Design: Solid stainless steel shafts with precision-pitched inner and outer ribbons.
  • Drive Systems: Heavy-duty gear motors, chain drives, or direct-drive alignments.
  • Discharge Mechanisms: Manual slide gates or pneumatically actuated butterfly valves.

Industry Applications

Engineered for extreme durability and easy sanitation, our blenders handle some of the most demanding bulk materials across various sectors:

  • Food Processing: Bakery premixes, spice blends, protein powders, and trail mixes.
  • Nutraceuticals: Vitamin powders, dietary supplements, and herbal extracts.
  • Plastics & Chemicals: Resin blending, colorant mixing, and dry chemical compounding.
  • Construction: Cement, grout, and dry mortar mixes.
