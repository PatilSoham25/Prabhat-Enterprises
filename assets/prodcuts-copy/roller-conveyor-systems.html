Roller Conveyor Systems

Efficient & Scalable Material Flow

Our industrial roller conveyor systems are designed to automate and streamline the movement of heavy goods, pallets, and packaged products across your facility. By significantly reducing manual lifting and carrying, these systems increase throughput, minimize product damage, and drastically enhance workplace safety.

Whether you need a simple gravity-fed decline to move lightweight boxes or a fully integrated, motor-driven live roller (MDR) system for zero-pressure accumulation of heavy pallets, we engineer modular solutions that perfectly adapt to your operational workflow and facility layout.

Technical Specifications

  • Roller Materials: Galvanized steel, 304/316L stainless steel, or heavy-duty PVC for sensitive or corrosive environments.
  • Drive Types: Gravity (non-powered), Motorized Driven Roller (MDR), and Chain-Driven Live Roller (CDLR) for massive loads.
  • Load Capacities: Engineered for varied applications, from lightweight package handling (50 lbs/ft) to extreme heavy-duty pallet handling (4,000+ lbs).
  • Frame Construction: Powder-coated formed steel or thick structural steel channels for maximum rigidity under dynamic loads.
  • Custom Features: Pop-up transfers, pneumatic stops, merge/divert sections, and adjustable structural support legs.

Industry Applications

Built for continuous, high-volume operation, our conveyor systems are the backbone of modern production and logistics:

  • Warehousing & Distribution: Sorting, accumulating, and directing packages rapidly to shipping bays.
  • Manufacturing: Assembly line progression and heavy machine-part transport between automated workstations.
  • Food & Beverage: Washdown-rated stainless steel gravity conveyors for moving packed crates and kegs safely.
  • Automotive: Heavy-duty CDLR systems specifically designed for transporting tires, engine blocks, and steel chassis components.
