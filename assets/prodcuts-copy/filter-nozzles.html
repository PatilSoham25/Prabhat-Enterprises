Industrial Filter Nozzles

Filter Nozzles

Optimal Liquid-Solid Separation

Our industrial filter nozzles are engineered to provide maximum fluid flow while completely retaining treatment media, such as sand, activated carbon, or ion-exchange resins. Designed to be mounted on tube sheets or false bottoms in filter vessels, they act as the primary gatekeepers of fluid purity.

Utilizing high-strength wedge wire or precision-molded designs, these nozzles resist clogging and withstand high differential pressures. They ensure a uniform collection and distribution of liquids, preventing media channeling and maximizing the efficiency of your filtration system.

Technical Specifications

  • Material Options: Stainless Steel (304, 316, 316L) for high temps, or heavy-duty Polypropylene/ABS for corrosive environments.
  • Slot Sizes: Precision-machined continuous slots ranging from extremely fine 0.1mm up to 2.0mm.
  • Thread Types: NPT, BSP, or custom metric threaded bases for secure, leak-proof mounting.
  • Configurations: Single-slot, double-slot, long-stem, and specialized backwash-friendly designs.
  • Flow Rates: Optimized open-area ratios to minimize pressure drop while handling high-volume throughput.

Industry Applications

Built for extreme durability and precise filtration, our nozzles are critical components in large-scale processing facilities:

  • Water Treatment: Sand filtration, carbon purification, and municipal water desalination plants.
  • Power Generation: Ion-exchange columns and condensate polishing systems.
  • Petrochemical & Refining: Catalyst retention vessels and heavy fluid filtration.
  • Food & Beverage: Liquid sugar filtration, brewing clarification, and cooking oil processing.
