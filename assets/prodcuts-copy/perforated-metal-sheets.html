Perforated Metal Sheets

Versatile Filtration & Airflow

Our perforated metal sheets are manufactured using advanced CNC punching technology to deliver precise hole patterns, uniform open areas, and superior structural integrity. These sheets are essential for applications requiring controlled passage of air, liquid, light, or sound.

Beyond their functional strength in industrial sorting and filtration, perforated metals offer an exceptional strength-to-weight ratio. They are heavily utilized not only as protective barriers and acoustic dampeners but also as striking architectural elements in modern building designs.

Technical Specifications

  • Material Options: Stainless Steel (304/316), Mild Steel, Aluminum, and Galvanized Iron.
  • Hole Patterns: Staggered round, straight round, square, slotted, hexagonal, and custom decorative.
  • Sheet Thickness: From extremely fine 0.5mm foil gauges up to heavy-duty 20mm load-bearing plates.
  • Open Area: Variable percentages (ranging from 10% to 80%) optimized for specific flow rates and structural loads.
  • Margins: Available with solid unperforated margins (standard or custom width) or no margins.

Industry Applications

Combining heavy-duty performance with aesthetic flexibility, our perforated sheets serve a wide array of specialized industries:

  • Industrial Processing: Filtration screens, sieves, and vibrating shaker screens for aggregate sorting.
  • Acoustics & HVAC: Soundproofing panels, ventilation grilles, and exhaust diffusion.
  • Safety & Protection: Heavy-duty machine guards, radiator covers, and anti-slip walkways.
  • Architecture: Building facades, sunshades, and decorative ceiling panels.
