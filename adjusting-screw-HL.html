Adjusting Screw HL

High-Load Structural Adjustments

Heavy-Duty Load Distribution

The Adjusting Screw HL (High-Load) is engineered for the most demanding structural alignment and leveling applications. This robust fastener is designed to support and precisely adjust massive static and dynamic loads, ensuring stable positioning for heavy machinery and structural frameworks.

Manufactured with an emphasis on shear strength and axial load capacity, the HL series frequently incorporates a specialized swivel pad or a hardened flat tip. This design allows for even weight distribution across uneven surfaces, preventing point-loading damage while maintaining pinpoint accuracy during the leveling process.

Technical Specifications

  • Material: Forged Carbon Steel or high-grade Stainless Steel for exceptional load-bearing capacity.
  • Load Capacity: Rated for extreme static loads, often exceeding 10,000 lbs depending on the specific thread diameter and pitch.
  • Thread Design: Coarse threads (UNC/Metric Coarse) optimized for strength and resistance to stripping under heavy tension.
  • Head Style: Oversized hexagonal or square heads for maximum wrench engagement and high-torque adjustments.
  • Base Integration: Available with articulated swivel pads to accommodate angled surfaces up to 15 degrees.

Industry Applications

The Adjusting Screw HL is the standard choice for scenarios where precision meets immense weight:

  • Machine Tooling: Leveling feet for massive CNC lathes, milling machines, and industrial presses.
  • Construction & Structural: Temporary support and final alignment of steel girders and pre-cast concrete elements.
  • Conveyor Systems: Precision height adjustment and leveling for heavy-duty material handling tracks.
  • Power Generation: Alignment of large generators, turbines, and heavy pump skids on uneven foundations.
