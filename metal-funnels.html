Industrial Metal Funnels

Metal Funnels

Safe & Hygienic Material Transfer

Our industrial metal funnels are engineered for the rapid, spill-free transfer of bulk liquids, heavy slurries, and fine powders. Unlike plastic funnels that can degrade, warp, or leach chemicals over time, our stainless steel funnels provide a permanent, non-reactive solution for rigorous manufacturing environments.

Designed with smooth, crevice-free interiors and precision-welded spouts, these funnels eliminate product buildup and cross-contamination. Whether you are batching volatile solvents or funneling dry food ingredients into a blender, they ensure a safe, efficient, and perfectly hygienic transfer process.

Technical Specifications

  • Material Construction: 304 and 316L Pharmaceutical-Grade Stainless Steel, or heavy-gauge Aluminum.
  • Spout Designs: Straight, angled, flanged, or custom tri-clamp compatible spouts for secure vessel attachment.
  • Capacity & Sizing: Ranging from small 1-pint laboratory funnels to massive 50-gallon bulk transfer hoppers.
  • Surface Finishes: Standard mill finish, brushed, or ultra-sanitary mirror polish for easy clean-in-place (CIP) operations.
  • Optional Features: Internal filtration screens, venting ribs to prevent airlocks, and heavy-duty side handles.

Industry Applications

Built for durability and chemical resistance, our metal funnels are the standard for high-volume transfer across strict industries:

  • Food & Beverage: Transferring spices, flour, brewing ingredients, and hot cooking oils.
  • Pharmaceuticals: Batching sterile powders, active pharmaceutical ingredients (APIs), and medical liquids.
  • Chemical Processing: Safely pouring corrosive acids, volatile solvents, and industrial lubricants.
  • Automotive & Aviation: Heavy-duty fluid management, oil changes, and fuel transfer without static buildup.
