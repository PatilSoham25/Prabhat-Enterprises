Industrial Wire Mesh

Wire Mesh

Precision Filtration & Structural Strength

Our industrial wire mesh provides the perfect balance of structural integrity and precise open-area airflow. Manufactured using state-of-the-art weaving looms and automated welding machines, our mesh guarantees consistent aperture sizes for the most demanding separation, filtration, and protection tasks.

Whether you require a heavy-duty welded grid for load-bearing applications or an ultra-fine micron woven mesh for chemical sieving, our stainless steel wire cloth delivers exceptional resistance to corrosion, high temperatures, and mechanical wear.

Technical Specifications

  • Material Options: 304/316L Stainless Steel, Galvanized Steel, Brass, Copper, and high-nickel alloys.
  • Mesh Types: Woven wire cloth (flexible, fine filtration) and Welded wire mesh (rigid, structural support).
  • Weave Patterns: Plain weave, twill weave, plain dutch weave, and twill dutch weave for absolute micron ratings.
  • Aperture/Mesh Count: Ranging from heavy 1-inch square openings down to ultra-fine 500+ mesh counts for sub-micron filtration.
  • Wire Diameter: Customizable wire gauges optimized for specific burst pressures and flow rates.

Industry Applications

Due to its incredible versatility, our wire mesh is heavily utilized across both heavy industry and highly specialized manufacturing:

  • Separation & Filtration: Extruder screens, liquid filtration discs, and fine powder sieving in pharmaceuticals.
  • Mining & Aggregate: Heavy-duty vibrating shaker screens for sorting rocks, gravel, and sand.
  • Architecture & Security: Machine guards, security fencing, animal enclosures, and decorative building facades.
  • Petrochemical: Catalyst support grids and high-temperature gas filtration.
