Technical Insight: The Anchor of the Engine

In high-performance and commercial engines, the valvetrain is subjected to immense cyclical stress millions of times per hour. The arm stud is the critical anchor that secures the rocker arm to the cylinder head, acting as the primary pivot point.

If a stud flexes or fails, it alters valve timing or causes catastrophic engine damage. By manufacturing in Pune—a global center for automotive excellence—we leverage advanced metallurgical testing to ensure our arm studs provide rigid, unyielding support, keeping your valvetrain geometry perfectly intact.
