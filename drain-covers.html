Drain Covers

Hygiene & Load-Bearing Safety

Our industrial drain covers are engineered to provide exceptional drainage efficiency while maintaining rigorous hygiene standards and superior load-bearing capacity. Designed to sit flush with your facility floor, they prevent tripping hazards while allowing high volumes of wastewater, chemicals, and debris to safely exit the workspace.

Unlike standard commercial grates, our heavy-duty covers are specifically fabricated to withstand the punishing environment of industrial washdowns, high-temperature fluid disposal, and the constant, heavy traffic of forklifts and pallet jacks.

Technical Specifications

  • Material Construction: Premium 304 or 316L Stainless Steel for maximum corrosion and chemical resistance.
  • Load Ratings: Ranging from pedestrian duty to extreme heavy-duty (capable of supporting fully loaded forklifts).
  • Surface Profiles: Anti-slip serrated, slotted, perforated mesh, or smooth bar grating.
  • Dimensions: Available in standard trench widths or custom-fabricated to fit existing floor troughs and catch basins.
  • Hygiene Design: Crevice-free, fully welded construction to prevent bacterial buildup and ensure easy sanitization.

Industry Applications

Built for environments where sanitation and safety are non-negotiable, our drain covers are heavily utilized across demanding sectors:

  • Food & Beverage Processing: Meat packing, dairy plants, breweries, and commercial kitchens requiring daily high-pressure washdowns.
  • Pharmaceuticals: Cleanrooms and sterile manufacturing areas requiring chemical-resistant drainage.
  • Chemical Processing: Handling corrosive runoff and heavy industrial effluents safely.
  • Manufacturing & Automotive: Facilities requiring heavy machinery and forklift traffic over trench drains.
